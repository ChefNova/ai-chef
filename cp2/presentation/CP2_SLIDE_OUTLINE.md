# ChefNova CP2 Presentation — Final 7-Slide Structure

## Slide 1 — Title & recap (~30 sec)
**Message:** ChefNova moves from grocery evidence to a feasible meal while keeping the user in control of inventory.

Show: title, team, GitHub, one-sentence problem.

## Slide 2 — Validation goals + theory claim (~45–60 sec)
Show: CP1 assumptions under test, the Gonzalez complementarity claim, human/AI/application role split, and one assumption that changed or was confirmed by evidence.

## Slide 3 — Prompting study design (~1–1.5 min)
Show: AI platforms, typical/edge/failure scenarios, controlled-prompt procedure, and reasoning/memory/attention/meta-coordination tags.

## Slide 4 — Evidence: worked and failed (~1.5–2 min)
Show actual screenshots/transcripts. Use at least one successful behavior and representative failures. Each example should identify the failure and why it matters.

## Slide 5 — Gap analysis (~1–1.5 min)
Each member briefly reports one speed-dating finding. Present the strongest examples as:

**failure receipt → theory → design response**

## Slide 6 — Refined concept + UI (~1–1.5 min)
Demonstrate: receipt → inventory review → meal request → constraints → recommendation → refinement. Explicitly state what changed because of CP2 evidence.

## Slide 7 — Risks + path to CP3 (~45 sec)
Show remaining risks, safeguards, and the planned human-alone vs AI-alone vs hybrid evaluation.
